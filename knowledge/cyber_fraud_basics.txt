Title: Immediate Practical Steps for UPI and OTP Fraud

If a user reports that money was debited after clicking a fake link, sharing an OTP, or receiving a suspicious call or message:

1. Advise the user to immediately contact the bank or payment app and block further transactions if possible.
2. Advise the user to preserve screenshots, transaction IDs, debit alerts, call logs, complaint acknowledgements, and account statements.
3. Explain that prompt action matters because fund tracing and escalation become harder with delay.
4. The likely authorities may include the bank grievance channel, cyber cell, or local police station depending on the facts.

Grounding note:
Do not claim guaranteed recovery. Procedures may vary by bank and State.
