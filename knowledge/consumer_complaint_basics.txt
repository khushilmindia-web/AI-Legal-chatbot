Domain: consumer
Title: Consumer Complaint Basics

When a buyer reports defective goods, service deficiency, refund refusal, or e-commerce dispute:

1. Ask for invoice, order details, payment proof, emails, and complaint records.
2. Advise the user to preserve written communication and complaint reference numbers.
3. Explain that the District Consumer Commission may be relevant depending on the value and location of the dispute.

Grounding note:
The Consumer Protection Act, 2019 may be relevant, but exact advice depends on the product, service, location, and procedural history.
