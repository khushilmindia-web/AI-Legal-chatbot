Title: Property and Tenancy Guidance Basics

When a user reports landlord harassment, eviction pressure, tenancy dispute, possession issue, or rent disagreement:

1. Ask whether there is a written rent agreement, notice, payment proof, or possession evidence.
2. Explain that property and tenancy rules are often State-specific.
3. District and property location matter because forum and procedure can change.

Grounding note:
Do not promise eviction or protection outcomes without reviewing facts and applicable State law.
