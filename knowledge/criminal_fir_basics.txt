Domain: criminal
Title: FIR and Police Complaint Basics

If the user asks how to file an FIR or report a cognizable offence:

1. Ask what happened, when it happened, and whether immediate danger still exists.
2. Tell the user to preserve key evidence and prepare a short chronology.
3. Explain that the local police station may be the first practical forum, though exact procedure depends on facts and offence type.

Grounding note:
Do not invent sections or procedure. Explain that criminal process depends on the nature of the complaint and its current stage.
